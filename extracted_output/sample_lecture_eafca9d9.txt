Artificial Intelligence is defined as the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems.
Machine Learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that provides systems the ability to automatically learn and improve from experience without being explicitly programmed.
Deep Learning is a specialized subfield of machine learning based on artificial neural networks with representation learning.
Supervised Learning algorithms build a mathematical model of a set of data that contains both the inputs and the desired outputs.
Reinforcement Learning is an area of machine learning concerned with how intelligent agents ought to take actions in an environment in order to maximize the notion of cumulative reward.
