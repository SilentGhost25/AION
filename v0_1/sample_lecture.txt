Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA) is a digital transmission technology that allows multiple users to access a single radio-frequency channel by dividing the signal into different time slots. Satellite transponders use TDMA to manage multiple earth stations efficiently without mutual interference.

Frequency Division Multiple Access (FDMA) divides the transponder bandwidth into separate frequency channels for each station. Each earth station transmits on its assigned frequency band continuously. Code Division Multiple Access (CDMA) uses unique pseudo-random noise codes to allow multiple signals to share the frequency band simultaneously across the entire satellite communications spectrum.
